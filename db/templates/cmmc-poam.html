1. Purpose

This Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M) records each NIST SP 800-171 security requirement that {{client_name}} has not yet fully implemented, the weakness it creates, and the specific steps, owners, resources and dates to correct it. It supports CMMC CA.L2-3.12.2 (develop and implement plans of action to correct deficiencies and reduce or eliminate vulnerabilities) and is referenced from the System Security Plan (SSP).

Internally, every known deficiency should be tracked here. For CMMC status, however, only certain deficiencies may remain open at the time of an assessment (see section 2).

2. CMMC rules for POA&Ms (Level 2)

Under 32 CFR 170.21, an organization may receive a Conditional Level 2 status with open POA&M items only if all of the following are true at the time of the assessment:

  • Minimum score: the assessment score divided by the total number of Level 2 requirements is at least 0.8 — that is, at least 88 of 110 requirements are met.
  • Only 1-point requirements: no requirement on the POA&M has a point value greater than 1 under the DoD Assessment Methodology.
  • Encryption exception: SC.L2-3.13.11 (FIPS-validated cryptography) may be on the POA&M if encryption is employed but is not FIPS-validated (which scores as 3 points rather than 5).
  • Requirements that can never be on a POA&M: AC.L2-3.1.20 (external connections), AC.L2-3.1.22 (control public information), CA.L2-3.12.4 (System Security Plan), PE.L2-3.10.3 (escort visitors), PE.L2-3.10.4 (physical access logs) and PE.L2-3.10.5 (manage physical access devices).
  • 180-day closeout: all POA&M items must be closed and confirmed by a POA&M closeout assessment within 180 days of the Conditional CMMC Status Date. If not, the Conditional status expires. For a Level 2 (C3PAO) assessment, the closeout is performed by a C3PAO; for Level 2 (Self), the organization performs and records it in SPRS.
  • Affirmation: the Affirming Official submits an affirmation in SPRS upon Conditional status, upon Final status after closeout, and annually thereafter.

A POA&M is not permitted for CMMC Level 1; all 15 Level 1 requirements must be met.

Operational plan of action: separate from this POA&M, an operational plan of action may be used to document temporary deficiencies that arise after a requirement was implemented (for example, a failed control discovered during operation) and how they will be restored. Record those in section 5 so they are not confused with assessment POA&M items.
