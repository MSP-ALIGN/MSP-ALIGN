This worksheet helps prepare the annual CMMC Level 1 self-assessment under 32 CFR 170.15 and FAR 52.204-21. The official affirmation is submitted by the Affirming Official in the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS), not by signing this document. Review and tailor it; it is not legal advice.

1. Purpose

CMMC Level 1 applies to contractors that process, store or transmit Federal Contract Information (FCI) — information provided by or generated for the government under a contract that is not intended for public release (for example, contract details, pricing, schedules and non-public drawings), but not simple transactional information such as that needed to process payments. This worksheet records how {{client_name}} meets each of the 15 basic safeguarding requirements, supports the results entered in SPRS, and documents the annual affirmation.

2. Key rules

  • All 15 requirements must be fully Met. A Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M) is not allowed for Level 1.
  • The self-assessment is performed and the results entered in SPRS at least annually, and the status must be current before award of a contract requiring Level 1.
  • A senior official of the company (the Affirming Official) affirms in SPRS, at least annually, that the company has implemented and will continue to maintain the requirements.
  • The affirmation is a representation to the federal government. A knowingly false or reckless affirmation can create liability under the False Claims Act and other laws; the DoJ Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative actively pursues such cases. Affirm only what is supported by evidence.
  • Assessment methods (examine, interview, test) and objectives are described in the DoD CMMC Level 1 Assessment Guide.

3. Scope (FCI assets)
