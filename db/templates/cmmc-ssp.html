  • Security Protection Assets (SPA) — assets that provide security functions or capabilities for the assessment scope, whether or not they handle CUI. Assessed against the requirements relevant to the security functions they provide.
    [e.g. firewall, identity provider/MFA, EDR/antivirus console, RMM tool, SIEM/log collector, backup system, password manager, {{company_name}} management tools and staff accounts]
  • Contractor Risk Managed Assets (CRMA) — assets that can, but are not intended to, process, store or transmit CUI because of the policies, procedures and practices in place. Must be documented in this SSP with the risk-based controls that keep CUI off them; the assessor may do limited checks.
    [e.g. general office PCs on the corporate network that are blocked from the CUI enclave, and the controls that prevent CUI from reaching them]
  • Specialized Assets — assets that can process CUI but cannot be fully secured: IoT/IIoT, operational technology (OT), government-furnished equipment, restricted information systems and test equipment. Documented in this SSP with how they are managed; not assessed against other requirements.
    [e.g. CNC machines, CMM inspection equipment, lab/test equipment, GFE laptops]
  • Out-of-Scope Assets — assets that cannot process, store or transmit CUI and do not provide security protection for CUI Assets, or are physically or logically separated from CUI Assets. An asset that fits any in-scope category cannot be out of scope. Describe the separation.
    [e.g. guest Wi-Fi, separately segmented shop-floor kiosks, personal phones with no access to CUI]
