# Code of conduct

MSP-ALIGN is a project for people who look after other people's IT. We want working on it to feel the way good client
work feels: respectful, patient and focused on the problem.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people here are busy techs helping in their spare time.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people. Explain your reasoning, and accept that the answer can be no.
- Welcome newcomers and questions of every level.
- Respect privacy. Never post anyone's client data, credentials or personal details.
- Keep it on topic and free of advertising.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commits) and anywhere someone is representing the
project, such as a shared chat, a forum post or an event.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@mspalign.org**, or use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment. Reports are read by the
maintainers only and kept confidential. If a maintainer is involved, say so, and another maintainer will handle it.

## What happens

The maintainers will look at every report promptly and fairly, and may:

1. Explain privately what was wrong and ask for it not to happen again.
2. Remove or edit comments, commits or issues that break this code.
3. Ban someone from the project for a time or for good, for serious or repeated problems.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
